1st Place Winner!
UX/UI Designer International Hackathon

Digital ID & Smart Hotel Key Concept

To challenge myself outside of standard academic projects, I recently participated in an intensive International Bootcamp / Hackathon. Our team proposed a digital innovation app that transforms a smartphone into a digital ID, driver's license, and smart hotel room key.

The core idea was to revolutionize the hotel check-in experience—making it fast, secure, and completely paperless without the need for traditional keycards, while integrating exclusive hotel privileges into a single platform.

Working under immense time pressure, I took on the role of Lead UX/UI Designer. I was responsible for driving the entire design phase from end to end, including conducting rapid User Research, structuring the User Flow, and crafting the High-Fidelity Prototype on Figma.

I am incredibly proud that our hard work and late nights paid off, leading our team to win the 1st Place Award! (Official award ceremony on the 24th).
